This add-on is 100% optional. You are fully welcome to move things yourself, set up, tear down, and reset the space on your own to keep your costs down. We built WhiteWall to be as affordable as possible by removing any unnecessary overhead. By keeping it self-service, we keep the labor cost down, which gives you immediate savings. However, if you would rather have the convenience instead of the savings and you would rather not do that work, our crew will gladly handle it for you.

What's Included

  • Before your event, we will meet on site, FaceTime, or text to plan exactly what stays in the space and what gets moved to storage. Anything that can't fit is tucked into the corner and hidden.
  • Crew tears down the standard studio floor plan before you arrive
  • Your rented items (tables, chairs, PA system, TV, rolling walls) staged in the middle of the space, straps off, ready to take off the dolly and place wherever you like, whenever you walk in
Where this add-on really pays off
  • When your event ends, leave everything exactly where it is
  • Full pack-up, reset, and cleanup of the space after your event

Clean up what you brought in, take out the trash, and leave the chairs, tables, and add-ons right where they are. No packing up the chairs and tables and loading them back onto the dollies, no rolling the walls back into the storage building, and no moving the furniture back into position. We pack it all up, bring the furniture back in, set the space back up, and reset everything for the next session, so you don't have to. You don't worry about a thing.

What's Not Included

  • Physically placing out the chairs, tables, and decor in their final spots (you take them off the dollies, which are already in there for you, ready to go, and arrange your specific setup)
  • Decorating (balloons, florals, linens, signage)
  • Staffing during your event itself. It is still self-service, we just help out with the annoying logistics.
