1. Core Intent

Controlled Fill Campaign

Create a controlled last-minute email/text campaign that helps WhiteWall fill underbooked weekend studio time without discounting days that are already performing well.

Key principle: discounts are used only when a specific studio on a specific weekend day is underbooked enough to justify promoting it. The offer should feel like rare last-minute availability — not a permanent or predictable discount program.

3. Discount Scope

Studio Time Only

The discount applies only to raw studio time.

Applies To

  • New bookings only
  • Base/raw studio-time charge only
  • The exact qualifying studio and exact qualifying date

Does Not Apply To

  • Add-ons, cleaning fees, equipment fees, or other services
  • Existing bookings or past bookings
  • Future weekends outside the campaign window
  • Anything other than base studio time
Suggested customer language: “Offer applies to new bookings only and applies to studio time only. It cannot be applied to existing reservations, add-ons, cleaning fees, or other charges.”

4. Day-by-Day Qualification Logic

Saturday + Sunday Only

Qualification is calculated per studio, per day. Friday does not count toward qualification analysis.

Analyzed Days

  • Saturday
  • Sunday
  • Friday is excluded from analysis

Analyzed Time Window

  • 7:30 AM – 6:30 PM
  • Only this window counts toward booked/open-hour totals
  • Outside hours should not affect qualification
A specific studio/date qualifies only if it has both:
1) fewer than 5 booked hours between 7:30 AM and 6:30 PM, and
2) 4 or more open usable hours between 7:30 AM and 6:30 PM.
ScenarioResult
Flagship Saturday has 6 booked hoursNo Flagship Saturday discount
Flagship Sunday has 3 booked hours and 6 open hoursFlagship Sunday qualifies
Taylor’s Mill Saturday has 2 booked hours and 7 open hoursTaylor’s Mill Saturday qualifies
Taylor’s Mill Sunday has 5 booked hoursTaylor’s Mill Sunday does not qualify
Saturday is slammed but Sunday is openSunday-only campaign/code can be sent
No minimum session length: one-hour openings are acceptable. There is no requirement for a 2-hour block or longer continuous opening.

5. Coupon Code Rules

Unique Every Weekend

Every campaign should use brand-new coupon codes that are tied to the specific studio, day/date, weekend, and discount level.

25% Tuesday Examples

  • FS-SAT-MAY30-25
  • FS-SUN-MAY31-25
  • TM-SAT-MAY30-25
  • TM-SUN-MAY31-25

50% Friday Examples

  • FS-SAT-MAY30-50
  • FS-SUN-MAY31-50
  • TM-SAT-MAY30-50
  • TM-SUN-MAY31-50

Restrictions

  • Unique to that specific weekend/date
  • Unique to the specific studio/day when possible
  • One use per user/customer
  • Non-stackable with other promos
  • Not reusable later
  • Disabled/expired after the campaign window
  • Valid only for new bookings
  • Valid only for raw studio time
  • Invalid for existing reservations
  • Invalid for add-ons, cleaning fees, or other charges
Expiration: coupons expire Sunday at 12:00 PM. A Sunday coupon may still apply to a Sunday evening session, but redemption must happen before noon Sunday.

6. Tuesday 25% Campaign

Drew Approval Required

Every Tuesday, Watson/system analyzes the coming weekend and prepares the initial 25% studio-time offer.

Check These Four Buckets

  • Flagship Saturday
  • Flagship Sunday
  • Taylor’s Mill Saturday
  • Taylor’s Mill Sunday

Calculate For Each

  • Booked hours
  • Open usable hours
  • Recommended available time slots
  • Qualification status
  • Proposed coupon code
Not automatic: before anything sends, Watson texts Drew with a full summary and waits for approval. Suggested instruction: Reply YES to send, NO to skip, or EDIT with changes.

Approval summary should include

  • Qualifying studio/date combinations
  • Non-qualifying studio/date combinations
  • Booked hours per studio/day
  • Open usable hours per studio/day
  • Recommended time slots
  • Proposed coupon codes
  • Proposed audience/list
  • Email/text summary
  • Studio-time-only confirmation
  • Existing-bookings-excluded confirmation

7. Friday 50% Escalation

Second Approval Required

On Friday morning, Watson/system re-checks the weekend. If qualifying availability still exists, Watson proposes a 50% studio-time escalation campaign.

Friday approval should include analytics: what changed since Tuesday, bookings created, coupon usage, email opens/clicks, text responses/clicks if available, and revenue/bookings attributed to Tuesday’s campaign.
  • Updated availability by studio/date
  • What changed since Tuesday
  • Bookings after Tuesday campaign
  • Whether Tuesday codes were used
  • Email open rate
  • Email click rate
  • Text response/click rate
  • Revenue/bookings attributed to Tuesday
  • Proposed 50% codes
  • Proposed copy summary
  • Recommended time slots

8. Audience / Recipient Rules

Same List for Email + Text

Email and text should go to the exact same list unless Drew later changes the rule.

Exclude

  • Anyone already booked for that same weekend
  • Unsubscribed contacts
  • Do-not-contact contacts
  • Problem customers if flagged
  • Anyone who should not receive promos

Existing Customer Language

Already booked? You’re all set with the time you chose. This last-minute offer is only for new bookings and cannot be applied to existing reservations.

9. Clean Workflow Summary

End-to-End
01

Tuesday analysis

Watson checks Flagship and Taylor’s Mill, analyzing Saturday and Sunday separately between 7:30 AM and 6:30 PM.

02

Qualification

A studio/date qualifies if it has fewer than 5 booked hours and 4+ open usable hours.

03

Approval request

Watson drafts the 25% campaign details and texts Drew for approval before anything sends.

04

Campaign launch

If approved, unique studio/date-specific 25% coupon codes are created and email/text go to the same approved list.

05

Friday re-check

Watson re-checks availability and performance, then asks Drew whether to send the 50% escalation.

06

Expiration

Coupons expire Sunday at noon and cannot be reused. Campaigns may keep accepting discounted bookings even if bookings climb above the original 5-hour threshold after launch.
