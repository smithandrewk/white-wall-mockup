Strategic takeaway: the easiest growth lever may be getting one-time clients to book their second session.

Methodology & Caveats

  • Data source: Square payments/orders available through the WhiteWall Square account.
  • Only completed, positive-value booking-like payments were counted as bookings.
  • Obvious add-on-only payments were excluded from booking counts where identifiable.
  • Customers were matched primarily by buyer email, then card fingerprint, then Square customer ID.
  • Names/emails are shown where Square had them available. Some older card-matched customers may not have a stored name or email.
  • Older Square transactions often have blank line-item names, so some bookings/add-ons were inferred or may be undercounted.
  • This is an operational analysis, not an audited financial report.
