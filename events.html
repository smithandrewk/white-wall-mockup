The space & capacity

How many people can I fit in there?

It depends on how you set it up. As a standing event the flagship holds up to 281 guests, a ceremony seats about 160, and a seated dinner runs around 80 at banquet tables with room left for an open dance floor. You can see every layout, drawn to scale, on the floor plans page.

Does it come with tables and chairs?

Yes. We keep 100 chairs and 10 eight-foot tables on site and available for your event, no rental run required.

What will the space look like when we show up?

However you want it to. The studio can be left in its everyday setup, or cleared completely into the storage building for a fully empty, blank-canvas event space. The gallery shows it in real life, and the floor plans show the exact footprint.

How many bathrooms are there?

There is a private restroom on site at the flagship (you can spot it on the floor plans).

Planning for a bigger group? Message us and we will walk you through the setup for your headcount.
Is it soundproof?

We will confirm the exact sound setup for your event before you book, including music and timing.

Planning your event

Can I host my kind of event there?

Almost certainly. The space regularly hosts bridal and baby showers, birthday parties, workshops and classes, corporate gatherings, launch parties, small weddings, and content shoots. If you have something else in mind, just ask, we are flexible.

Can I have food and drink there?

Yes, food and drink are welcome, it is a big part of what the space is for. If you are bringing a caterer or planning something specific, let us know and we will make sure everything is set for your date.

We will finalize the catering and beverage details with you as we lock in your event.
Can I set up the night before?

Early access depends on what is on the calendar around your date. Tell us your timeline and we will work out setup, and a night-before window when it is available, with you.

Exact setup windows are something we confirm per booking.
Are there multi-day event options?

Yes. You can book the space across multiple days for a single event. Tell us your dates and we will put it together.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on your date, how many hours you need, and your guest count. The quickest way to see the exact price for your slot is to start a booking, you will see live pricing before you pay anything. One thing to know up front: events with 35 or more guests include a $150 cleaning fee.
